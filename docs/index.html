Track it your way.

Record your expenses

Save a title, amount, category, and date for each expense. Add a note or edit the details later.

Review your spending

Filter by today, this week, this month, or category. See the total and top category for the expenses you’re viewing.

Make it yours

Create categories with your own colors and icons. Choose from ten currency display formats and switch between light, dark, and system appearance.

Available for Android 8.0 and newer

Expenses and categories are stored locally. The app works offline.
